West Wall

Morning

The sun comes up,
The sky turns bright,
A brand new day
Replaces night.

Rain

Raindrops fall,
Tap the ground,
Making music
All around.

East Wall

The Moon

The moon is high,
Above the trees,
Shining softly
In the breeze.

Dream

I close my eyes,
The world grows still,
And dreams appear
Beyond the hill.
